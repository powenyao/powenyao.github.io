A 3-hour LARP about game conventions with a Secret Santa-like mechanic. Low-conflict, wholesome, and reflective.
You are at Roles & Rolls, a beloved regional convention. You want to give back to the community.
You will make a character, meet one another, share what will make the convention awesome, and decide who you’d like to help.
Next, explore the gifts' impact: react and share how the gifts shaped the convention and your experience.
Come to this meta-convention to share your experiences and hopes for this convention's past, present, and future.
