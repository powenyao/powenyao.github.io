# Motivation

When I went from MS student in Computer Science general track to a PhD student at USC and responsible for being the
teaching assistant for the senior capstone class, I figured that I can perform my role better if I have learned the same
material that they did.
I took CTIN 488 Game Design Workshop - the introductory game design course that all computer science games MS students
had to take.
