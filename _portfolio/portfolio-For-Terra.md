Factions cooperate to terraform and colonize planets for Terra, humanity's home planet, while competing for fame. With each successive competition, players accumulate resources that can unlock higher-tier research and abilities, which enable them to take on greater challenges.

Mechanically, it is a tableau-building board game inspired by Terraforming Mars: Ares Expedition. Progression-wise, it is based on Legacy and Campaign games, such as Pandemic Legacy, Gloomhaven, and Arkham Horror the Card Game.
