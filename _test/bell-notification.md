I was curious and did an Allergy Blood Test at [長庚安醫事檢驗所 Chang-Geng-Ann Examination Center](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RRBn7cgWUSM94nha6).



The [Blood Test "123種過敏原”](https://www.medicallab-e.com.tw/product1.html) claims to test for 123 types of acute Allergen (IgE) and 101 types of slow Allergen (IgG).

While I knew I had dust mite allergy for a while, it turns out that there are many more allergen that impacts me and dust mites aren't even the worst offender. 

The test results are presented in a basic infographics. Lv 5 being the most serious while Lv 1 being the least serious.

On IgE Results
=

* lv 4 cat hair
* lv 3 dust mite
* lv 2 lamb hair/skin, mutton, cashew
* lv 1 Beef

[IgE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunoglobulin_E) or Immunoglobulin E is a type of antibody and the presence of allergen specific IgE can suggest a specific allergy.
While my friend had previously stayed over with cats and I sneezed and itched. I thought it was just because the cats tend to go all over the house, collecting dust and bringing them close to me. My prior diagnosed dust mite allergy led me to misattribute my allergic reactions. Fortunately, I don't have a lethal reaction to any of these allergens.

I also find it interesting that my favorite type of nuts - cashews - are the one type that I am allergic and no others. Perhaps I have consumed too much cashews along with beef. 

On IgG Results
=
* lv 5 cod, eel, cashew
* lv 4 milk, egg white
* lv 3 salmon, grapefruit
* lv 2 lamb, shrimp, wheat
* lv 1 cheese, beef, clam, corn, garlic


[IgG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immunoglobulin_G) or Immunoglobulin G is also a type of antibody.
The current research seems to suggest that the IgG does not have any relationship to food intolerances. 
At the same time, my test results for IgG response seems to have a high correlation with food I enjoy eating. 

This is likely why some refer to IgG testing as 智商稅 (IQ Tax) as its not backed by current science. The results also tend to have so many food items that it makes figuring out what to eat a chore. 

Thoughts
=

All in all, I think the allergy blood test (IgG aside) was illuminating. Now I know to watch out and follow up on potential cat allergy. While I have low level of allergy toward lamb, cashew, and beef, I am not too worried about consuming them. I could do food challenges to try eliminating them from my diet if opportunity arises. I also can reduce the consumption of beef since it follows the guideline of reducing consumption of red meat.

At the same time, the test results did raise a bunch of questions and more learning.
[Society of Surgical Oncology](https://surgonc.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/SSO-5things-List_2020-Updates-11-2020.pdf) suggested "Don’t perform unproven diagnostic tests, such as immunoglobulin G(IgG) testing or an indiscriminate battery of immunoglobulin E (IgE)tests, in the evaluation of allergy."
